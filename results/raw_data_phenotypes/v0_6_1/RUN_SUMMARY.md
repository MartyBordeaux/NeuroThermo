## Corrected primary definition

Firing rate and mean ISI form one excitability/timing domain. Predictive information forms a second independent domain. A strict exit requires both domain scores and their mean burden to exceed z=2.0, a target-specific nested leave-one-out WT rank p <= 0.1, and persistence for 2 adjacent current levels. Non-exits are censored at 650 pA.
