# Data inventory

This directory contains the raw electrophysiology recordings, exact frozen analysis inputs, imported frozen result/checkpoint trees for computationally expensive stages, and publication-facing source data.

## Raw recordings and QC provenance

The release includes all 50 current-clamp ABF files used by the project snapshot:

- `raw/WT/`: 32 ABF files corresponding to the WT recording set;
- `raw/SCA3/`: 18 ABF files corresponding to the SCA3 recording set.

Per-file sizes and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `../docs/RAW_DATA_MANIFEST.tsv`. The exact QC/provenance workbook is stored under `qc/`, and the final fixed-QC source is under `../code/pipelines/NeuroThermo_stage1_qc_fixed/`.

The raw file count is not the biological-cell count because the recording set contains both current-clamp (`cc_*.abf`) and capacitance (`capa_*.abf`) files.

## Cohort and animal provenance

`animal_id_recovery/accepted_cohort.csv` is the canonical accepted-cohort animal mapping used by the publication replay. The older top-level `animal_to_cell_mapping.csv` is retained as a publication-facing summary, not as the source of truth for the characterization compatibility layer.

The primary multi-sweep analysis cohort contains 18 fitted cells: 12 WT and 6 SCA3. Where exact animal recovery is supported, the accepted-cohort map records it explicitly. Four WT recordings remain unresolved at the animal-identity level; recording-date labels used in Fig.1 for those cells are derived from their raw ABF headers and are not treated as recovered animal IDs.

The SCA3 current-clamp material maps to two animal-days (`DD20` and `DD24`). The limited biological replication is reported as a design limitation; cell-level analyses do not imply genotype-population inference at the animal level.

## Data levels

- `raw/`: original ABF recordings included in the publication snapshot.
- `qc/`: QC/provenance workbook and its fingerprint.
- `calibration_bundle/`: immutable server calibration archive.
- `calibration/`: deterministic extraction of accepted sweeps, manual peak overrides, threshold brackets, baseline/seed tables, plus `CALIBRATION_PROVENANCE.tsv` generated by `code/prepare_calibration.py`.
- `v3_9_results_full/`: exact full v3.9 frozen result tree used for downstream clean-clone replay when the expensive optimizer is not rerun.
- `dynamic_v2_1_frozen/` and `endpoint_ensemble_v1_0_frozen/`: exact frozen input layers for downstream analyses.
- `transition_v1_0_frozen/` through `transition_v1_3_frozen/`: exact transition frozen inputs.
- `transition_v1_0_results/` through `transition_v1_3_results/`: exact transition result/checkpoint trees used for validation and downstream replay.
- `inputs/`: analysis-ready support-state inputs used by the KL and nonequilibrium publication packages.
- `figure_source/`: immutable publication-facing source tables. `code/assemble_figure_source.py` reconstructs the eight Fig.1--3 tables consumed by the canonical renderer and validates them against these references.
- `kl_convergence_v1_0_1/`: full-state and marginal KL outputs used by the manuscript.
- `nonequilibrium_geometry_v1_0_1/`: nonequilibrium/Fisher/time-reversal outputs used by the manuscript.

Publication-facing capacitance normalization is performed explicitly during source-data assembly rather than silently modifying the upstream frozen result trees.
